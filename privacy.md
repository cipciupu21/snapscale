# Privacy Policy for SnapScale

SnapScale ("the Extension") respects your privacy. 

- **Data Processing:** All image resizing, conversions, and screenshot captures happen entirely locally inside your browser using HTML Canvas. 
- **Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, log, or transmit any personal data, images, or browsing history to any external servers.
- **Permissions:** The extension uses `activeTab`, `downloads`, and `storage` solely to perform user-requested image downloads and store local preferences.
